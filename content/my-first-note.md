# Hello World!

This is my first note on my new Quartz blog. 

## Why Quartz is great:
* It supports **Markdown**.
* It turns folders into **navigation trees**.
* You can add links like [[this]] to connect notes together.

### Tips for writing:
* **Frontmatter:** The text between the `---` lines at the top is called "Frontmatter." It tells Quartz the title and metadata for your page.
* **Linking:** Use double brackets `[[Like this]]` to link to another note in your `content` folder. This is how you build a "digital garden."
